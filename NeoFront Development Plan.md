# NeoFront Development Plan

* Hexagonal map
* Army organization, armor, warship, and aircraft models:
  * Designed and selected before the match; only a limited set of organizations and models can be chosen during the match.
* Tiles:
  * Have base manpower, can hold 4 divisions
  * Can build a city, providing extra manpower and 6 building slots, can hold 6 divisions
	* Arsenal: produces equipment, aircraft
	* Shipyard: produces warships
	* Barracks: houses more troops
	* Airfield: houses aircraft
